# The Atlas Registry

Welcome to the Atlas Global Registry! This directory contains the master list of all independent decentralized networks and forks powered by the Kinetic protocol that have registered their TLDs (Top-Level Domains).

If you are running a Kinetic fork and want your domains to be accessible globally, [Register your Network here](https://github.com/saifmukhtar/kinetic-atlas/issues/new/choose).
